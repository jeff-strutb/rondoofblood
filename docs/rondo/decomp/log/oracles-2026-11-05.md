## A5 (`check.c`, `oracle.py a5`)

The original runs untouched; each call's RAM is rewound by undoing a log of its
writes, the C runs on the entry registers with hardware reads replayed, and
registers, flags, MPRs, RAM and hardware writes must match. The order of accesses is
checked too and reported separately. Code the C calls by address is replayed from
the log, with its entry registers compared.

Control: one wrong carry in `apply_axis`, on one script: 219,836 calls divergent, 5
routines DIVERGENT, each with the bytes that differ.

Causes of everything flagged, all in the C:

- `nz_from_x` left set after a handler returned, so a walker's exit took N and Z from
  X (`StreamWalkForward`, `StreamWalkBack`, `StreamEdgeLeft`).
- `JMP [zp]` reads its pointer: `RunStreamRecord` left out those two reads.

After the fixes: 7,800,129 calls, 7,431,332 identical, 0 divergent, 0 order flags,
368,797 unverified. Routines: 79 IDENTICAL, 9 UNVERIFIED, 30 never called as a call.

## A7 (`oracle.py a7`)

A recording run logs each call's cost in master clocks and a hash of RAM and the
picture per frame. The hybrid swaps in the C wherever the call had no interrupt in
it, did not cross a frame, and its C runs no original code, and charges the recorded
clocks less what the C's own bus accesses cost. The stack below S is not hashed.

First run: boot and attract identical; the four play scripts differed around frame
14,700. A dump showed only `$204F` live and different, and a clock check at every
call entry found the first drift after a call recorded as 4,294,791,259 clocks. The
core resets its timestamp inside a frame's run, not at its end, so a call could
cross that reset unflagged. A clock that went backward now marks the call as crossed.

## Where the routines stand

59 routines, 1,164 B, meet all of: a judged A5 with none divergent, every byte run,

## Limits of these oracles

- A7 cannot swap a routine that reaches `call_original` or `wait_interrupt` (31 of
  118), so those have only A5, and `FrameInterrupt`, the waits and the routines left
  through the stack have neither. Running original code from inside a swapped C is
  not built.
- A5 judges calls reached by JSR, BSR or a jump. A routine entered only by a branch
  or by running into it is covered as part of its caller, not on its own.
- The hybrid's bus accesses all happen at the call's first clock, and the rest of its
  cost is charged after. A VDC write landing early did not show in 119,770 frames of
  these scripts, but later raster effects may be sensitive to it.
- Stages past the first, Maria and the ending are not in the scripts.
